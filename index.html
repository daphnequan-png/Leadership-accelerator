Hi Leaders,

Introducing the Athena Leadership Accelerator—a 12-month experience for a select group of emerging people leaders ready to build toward Operations Director and senior leadership roles.

Reserved for the ten

Learn directly from Athena's VPs in 12 small-group sessions.
Have a dedicated VP mentor in your corner all year.
Own a real Athena challenge and defend your team's results before an executive panel.

One cohort. By nomination only.

Directors and above: Who is delivering exceptional impact and already stretching beyond their role? This is your opportunity to champion them.

Nominate an emerging leader →

Deadline: November 10, 2026, at noon EST.

A few things to know

Who's eligible? Regularized people leaders with a completed H1 rating of at least Meets Expectations, with no active PIP or open disciplinary case. Directors and above submit nominations.
What's the commitment? At least four hours a month for 12 months. The cohort begins the week of November 16.
Does it guarantee promotion? It builds readiness for senior roles; promotion is not guaranteed.
